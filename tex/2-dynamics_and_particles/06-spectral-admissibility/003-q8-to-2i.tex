\subsection{Effective-Amplitude Contrast between Spin Sectors of $2I$}
  \label{subsec:q8-to-2i}

  The binary icosahedral group $2I$ is a finite subgroup of $SU(2)$ and contains $Q_8$.
  Its spectrum depends on the generating set; a numerical contrast must specify that set.
  We use the union of the two order-10 conjugacy classes,
  $S=10a\cup10b$, with $|S|=24$, as in~\cite{Beau2026a1}.
  Since this symmetric set is a union of conjugacy classes, its adjacency operator is scalar
  on each irreducible sector.

  In the source's spin-sector notation, the Laplacian eigenvalues are
  $\lambda_{1/2}=18$ and $\lambda_{5/2}=28$.
  Under the single-mode, effective-amplitude and linearised-dispersion hypotheses of
  Section~\ref{subsec:admissibility-bounded-flux}, their windows satisfy
  \begin{equation}
    \frac{A_{1/2}^{\max}}{A_{5/2}^{\max}}
    = \sqrt{\frac{28}{18}} = \sqrt{\frac{14}{9}} \approx 1.247.
  \end{equation}
  This compares two spin sectors, not a non-abelian sector with an abelian one:
  $2I$ has no non-trivial one-dimensional representation.
  The spin-$3/2$ sector also has eigenvalue~18, so this contrast does not single out spin~$1/2$.
  Nor does the ratio select an occupation or a preferred realised sector.
  Other generating sets need not give these eigenvalues or the same ordering.

  Neutral generators, introduced next, impose a separate representation-theoretic condition.
  Their classification is not a consequence of this window comparison.
