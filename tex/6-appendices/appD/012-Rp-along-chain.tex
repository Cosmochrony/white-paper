\subsection{Effective-Amplitude Ratios for Specified Finite Graphs}
  \label{subsec:Rp-along-chain}

  The conditional envelope compares two specified positive-eigenvalue sectors by
  $A_\rho^{\max}/A_\sigma^{\max}=\sqrt{\lambda_\sigma/\lambda_\rho}$.
  The oscillatory sector, effective-amplitude convention and linearised dispersion of
  Section~\ref{subsec:admissibility-bounded-flux} are assumed throughout.

  \begin{center}
    \begin{tabular}{lcc}
      \hline
      Graph & Compared sectors & Window ratio \\
      \hline
      $Q_8$, $S=\{\pm i,\pm j,\pm k\}$ & dimension 2 / non-trivial dimension 1 & $\sqrt{4/3}$ \\
      $2I$, $S=10a\cup10b$ & spin $1/2$ / spin $5/2$ & $\sqrt{14/9}$ \\
      \hline
    \end{tabular}
  \end{center}

  The $2I$ generating set has size~24 and eigenvalues~18 and~28 for these two
  sectors~\cite{Beau2026a1}.
  It is not an abelian/non-abelian comparison; $2I$ has no non-trivial one-dimensional representation.
  The table compares different sector pairs and defines no evolution of one common ratio.
  A wider effective-amplitude window does not select a sector or its occupation.

  LPS graphs are not intermediate subgroups of $SU(2)$ in a chain of inclusions.
  No sector pair defining the same ratio on LPS graphs or on $SU(2)$ is specified here.
  No numerical LPS ratio, continuum limit equal to unity, or disappearance of the filter is asserted.
