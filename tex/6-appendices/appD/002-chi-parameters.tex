\subsection{Estimates of \texorpdfstring{$\chi$}{χ}-Field Parameters}
  \label{subsec:chi-parameters}

  Effective parameters characterize the projected admissibility operator and
  do not represent fundamental degrees of freedom.
  We distinguish bare parameters ($K_{0,\mathrm{bare}}$,
  $\chi_{c,\mathrm{bare}}$), which are universal substrate invariants,
  from effective parameters ($K_{0,\mathrm{eff}}$,
  $\chi_{c,\mathrm{eff}}$) encoding the local density of admissibility constraints
  (Section~\ref{subsec:renormalization-hbar}).

  No relation between these parameters and the gravitational constant is established in this paper
  (Appendix~\ref{subsec:fundamental-quantities}).
  No numerical value of $K_0$ or $\chi_c$ follows from a gravitational identification such as $\chi_c \sim \ell_P$
  or $\chi_c \sim c/H_0$, and no spectral self-similarity keeping $G$ constant across scales is established.
  In the convention of Appendix~\ref{subsec:fundamental-quantities} ($\chi_c$ a length, $K_0$ of dimension
  $\mathrm{s}^{-2}$), a determination of these constants requires a normalization of the substrate, which is left
  open; the electroweak calibration of $\chi_c$ is recorded in Appendix~\ref{sec:consistency_checks}.
