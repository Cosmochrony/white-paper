\subsection{Admissibility Envelope by Sector: $\mu_\rho$, $\lambda_\rho$, $A_\rho^{\max}$}
  \label{subsec:admissibility-envelope-by-sector}

  For a finite group $G$ with symmetric generating set $S$ of size $|S|$, the
  average Laplacian eigenvalue in irreducible sector $\rho$ is
  \begin{equation}
    \lambda_\rho \;=\; |S| - \frac{1}{\dim\rho}\sum_{s\in S}\chi_\rho(s)
    \;\equiv\; |S|\,\mu_\rho,
    \qquad
    \mu_\rho \;=\; 1 - \frac{1}{|S|\dim\rho}\sum_{s\in S}\chi_\rho(s).
  \end{equation}
  When the block is scalar (in particular for a union of conjugacy classes), $\lambda_\rho$ is its
  common eigenvalue.
  For $\lambda_\rho>0$, the conditional effective-amplitude envelope of
  Section~\ref{subsec:admissibility-bounded-flux} gives, in its single-mode linearised regime,
  \begin{equation}
    A_\rho^{\max} \;=\; \frac{c_{\mathrm{BI}}}{\sqrt{|S|\,\mu_\rho}}.
  \end{equation}

  For $Q_8$ with $S=\{\pm i,\pm j,\pm k\}$, $|S|=6$:

  \begin{center}
    \begin{tabular}{lcccc}
      \hline
      Sector $\rho$ & $\dim\rho$ & $\mu_\rho$ & $\lambda_\rho$ & $A_\rho^{\max}/c_{\mathrm{BI}}$ \\
      \hline
      $\rho_1$ (trivial) & 1 & 0 & 0 & unconstrained (zero mode) \\
      $\rho_2,\rho_3,\rho_4$ (abelian) & 1 & $4/3$ & 8 & $1/\sqrt{8}$ \\
      $\rho_s$ (spinorial) & 2 & 1 & 6 & $1/\sqrt{6}$ \\
      \hline
    \end{tabular}
  \end{center}

  The spinorial sector admits amplitude $1/\sqrt{6}$ versus $1/\sqrt{8}$ for the
  abelian sectors.
  The admissibility ratio is
  \begin{equation}
    R_p(Q_8) \;=\; \frac{A_{\rho_s}^{\max}}{A_{\rho_k}^{\max}}
    \;=\; \sqrt{\frac{8}{6}} \;=\; \sqrt{\frac{4}{3}} \;\approx\; 1.155.
  \end{equation}

  For $2I$ with $S=10a\cup10b$, $|S|=24$, the corresponding source values are
  $\lambda_{1/2}=18$ and $\lambda_{5/2}=28$~\cite{Beau2026a1}.
  Thus the effective-amplitude contrast between these spin sectors is
  \begin{equation}
    \frac{A_{1/2}^{\max}}{A_{5/2}^{\max}}
    =\sqrt{\frac{28}{18}}=\sqrt{\frac{14}{9}}\approx1.247.
  \end{equation}
  There is no non-trivial abelian sector in $2I$.
  This contrast depends on the specified generating set and does not imply dynamical selection
  (Section~\ref{subsec:q8-to-2i}).
