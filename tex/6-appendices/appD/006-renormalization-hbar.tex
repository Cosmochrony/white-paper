\subsection{Renormalization and the Universality of \texorpdfstring{$\hbar$}{ℏ}}
  \label{subsec:renormalization-hbar}

  We distinguish bare substrate parameters from their effective counterparts:
  \begin{equation}
    \hbar_\chi \equiv \frac{c^3} {K_{0,\mathrm{bare}}\,\chi_{c,\mathrm{bare}}}.
    \label{eq:hbar-chi}
  \end{equation}
  Effective parameters ($K_{0,\mathrm{eff}}$,
  $\chi_{c,\mathrm{eff}}$) incorporate the local density of admissibility constraints.
  No observable variation of $\hbar$ arises within a fixed projective epoch,
  since bare parameters remain invariant.
  Under the conditional identification $\hbar = M_\chi\hbar_\chi$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:hbar-Mchi}), the perceived
  universality of $\hbar$ stems from its exclusive dependence on bare quantities, invariant under projective scaling;
  the mass scale $M_\chi$ is not supplied in this paper.
