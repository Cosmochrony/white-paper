\subsection{The Newton coupling: an open matching problem}
\label{subsec:stepB-G}

The leading term of Eq.~\eqref{eq:BI-IR} has the form of the Einstein--Hilbert action
$\frac{1}{16\pi G_N}\int R\sqrt{-g}\,d^4x$ only once its coefficient is matched:
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\mathcal{N}\,\ell_{\mathrm{sp}}^{2}}{2} \;=\; \frac{1}{16\pi G_N}.
  \label{eq:G-prediction}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:G-prediction} is a matching condition, not a prediction.
It is written, as in~\cite{Beau2026h}, without factors of $c$.
It relates the observed Newton coupling $G_N$ to the combination $\mathcal{N}\,\ell_{\mathrm{sp}}^{2}$ of the two
constants $\mathcal{N}$ and $\ell_{\mathrm{sp}}$, which the completion conditions of Section~\ref{subsec:stepB-BI}
leave free.
In the companion gravity paper~\cite{Beau2026h}, the renormalised Einstein coefficient is likewise a matching
definition of the observed Newton coupling, not a prediction of its finite value.

No formula for $G_{\mathrm{eff}}$ in terms of $c_{\mathrm{BI}}$ and $\hbar_\chi$ alone is a prediction of this paper.
Such a formula would require a normalisation of the Born--Infeld action by $c_{\mathrm{BI}}$ and $\hbar_\chi$ that
is supplied by no construction in this paper, nor by the companion papers~\cite{Beau2026c,Beau2026h}.
In the convention of Appendix~\ref{subsec:fundamental-quantities}, $c_{\mathrm{BI}}$ and $\hbar_\chi$ carry only
length and time, so no combination of them has the dimension of $G$ without a further mass scale.

A prediction of the Newton coupling would require, at least:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
  \item a derivation of the Einstein coefficient from $S_{\mathrm{coh}}$, which Appendix~\ref{subsec:IR} leaves
  undetermined ($\kappa_{\mathrm{coh}}$);
  \item a determination of $\mathcal{N}$ and $\ell_{\mathrm{sp}}$, or of their product, in terms of substrate
  quantities;
  \item a dimensionally consistent relation between those substrate quantities and the observed constants.
\end{enumerate}
None of these steps is carried out here.
The schematic relation $G = G(c_{\mathrm{BI}}, \hbar_\chi, \ldots)$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:G-prediction2} remains a
target of such a construction.
