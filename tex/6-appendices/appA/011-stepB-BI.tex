\subsection{Saturation and the Born--Infeld candidate completion}
  \label{subsec:stepB-BI}

  This paper does not derive an Einstein--Hilbert term from the coherence functional
  $S_{\mathrm{coh}}$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:EH-result}: Appendix~\ref{subsec:IR} leaves the coefficient
  $\kappa_{\mathrm{coh}}$ of $R$ undetermined and records the operator-level construction that is missing.
  The present subsection addresses a separate, conditional question.
  Given an Einstein infrared term, which local tensorial completions are admissible when the curvature approaches
  the saturation regime?
  The answer used here is the classification of the companion gravity paper~\cite{Beau2026h}, restated with its
  hypotheses.
  It is a statement about the completion of a supplied Einstein term, not a derivation of that term, or of its
  completion, from $S_{\mathrm{coh}}$.

  \paragraph{Setup.}
    Following~\cite{Beau2026h}, let $\ell_{\mathrm{sp}}$ be the length of its spectral proper-time cutoff
    ($\Lambda = \ell_{\mathrm{sp}}^{-1}$), and define the mixed Ricci endomorphism
    \begin{equation}
      X^{\mu}{}_{\nu} = \ell_{\mathrm{sp}}^{2}\, g^{\mu\rho} R_{\rho\nu}.
      \label{eq:BI-mixed-ricci}
    \end{equation}
    The relation of $\ell_{\mathrm{sp}}$ to the substrate scales of this paper is not established here.
    A local covariant completer has density $\sqrt{-g}\,[\Phi(X) - 1]$, with $\Phi$ a smooth scalar function of the
    endomorphism.
    The conditions of~\cite{Beau2026h} are:
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(C\arabic*)]
      \item spectral covariance: $\Phi$ depends on $X$ only through its eigenvalues;
      \item Einstein infrared limit: $\Phi(X) = 1 + \tfrac12\,\mathrm{tr}\,X + \mathcal{O}(\|X\|^2)$ for weak
      curvature;
      \item algebraic Born--Infeld saturation: $\Phi$ is defined on the whole region where $I + X$ is positive and
      vanishes on its boundary, that is, as an eigenvalue of $I + X$ tends to zero with the remaining eigenvalues of
      $X$ in a compact subset of $(-1, +\infty)$;
      \item spectral isotropy: $\Phi$ is a symmetric function of the eigenvalues;
      \item coherence extensivity: $\log\Phi$ is additive over admissibly independent sectors.
    \end{enumerate}
    In~\cite{Beau2026h}, (C5) is the open Hypothesis~[H-ext]; it is not derived from Axiom~A3 of the
    Foundation paper~\cite{Beau2026m}, nor from the sector reading of that axiom.
    The capacity analysis of~\cite{Beau2026c} motivates a saturating, square-root type response; it does not
    formulate these gravitational conditions and does not fix the relation between $c_{\mathrm{BI}}$ and any
    continuum length, including $\ell_{\mathrm{sp}}$.

  \paragraph{Determinantal member.}
    The Born--Infeld candidate completion is
    \begin{equation}
      S_{\mathrm{coh}}^{\mathrm{full}}[g, \psi]
      = \mathcal{N} \int_{\mathcal{M}} \sqrt{-g}\,
      \left( \sqrt{\det\!\left( \delta^{\mu}{}_{\nu} + X^{\mu}{}_{\nu} \right)} - 1 \right) d^4x
      + S_{\mathrm{matter}}[g, \psi],
      \label{eq:BI-gravity}
    \end{equation}
    where the overall constant $\mathcal{N}$ is not fixed by the completion conditions.
    The eigenvalue algebra of~\cite{Beau2026h} is carried out on the Riemannian section; the form with
    $\sqrt{-g}$ presupposes a Lorentzian continuation that is not performed there.
    For $\|X\| \ll 1$,
    \begin{equation}
      \sqrt{\det(I + X)}
      = 1 + \tfrac12\,\mathrm{tr}\,X
      + \tfrac18\,(\mathrm{tr}\,X)^2 - \tfrac14\,\mathrm{tr}\,X^2
      + \mathcal{O}(\|X\|^3),
      \label{eq:BI-expand}
    \end{equation}
    that is, $\tfrac12\,\ell_{\mathrm{sp}}^2 R + \ell_{\mathrm{sp}}^4\bigl(\tfrac18 R^2
    - \tfrac14 R_{\mu\nu}R^{\mu\nu}\bigr)$ at the first two orders.
    Hence
    \begin{equation}
      S_{\mathrm{coh}}^{\mathrm{full}}
      = \frac{\mathcal{N}\,\ell_{\mathrm{sp}}^{2}}{2}
      \int_{\mathcal{M}} R \sqrt{-g}\, d^4x
      + S_{\mathrm{matter}}
      + \mathcal{O}\!\left(\ell_{\mathrm{sp}}^{4}\,\mathcal{R}^{2}\right),
      \label{eq:BI-IR}
    \end{equation}
    where $\mathcal{R}^{2}$ stands for the quadratic curvature invariants.
    The leading term is the supplied Einstein term only once its coefficient is matched
    (Section~\ref{subsec:stepB-G}).
    The infrared regime is $R\,\ell_{\mathrm{sp}}^{2} \ll 1$, with the qualifications on the renormalised
    coefficients stated in~\cite{Beau2026h}.

  \begin{proposition}[Admissibility and non-uniqueness of the Born--Infeld completion of a supplied Einstein term]
    \label{prop:BI-unique}
    Let the completer density be $\sqrt{-g}\,[\Phi - 1]$, with the argument of $\Phi$ a carrier of the family of
    \cite{Beau2026h}: an endomorphism obtained by raising one index of a local covariant symmetric tensor linear in
    the curvature and of two-derivative order, with spectrum not a multiple of the identity in general, normalised so
    that its trace is $\ell_{\mathrm{sp}}^{2} R$.
    Assume (C1)--(C4) and Hypothesis~[H-ext], with (C3) and (C5) applied to the curvature endomorphism $X$ and to
    its eigenvalue sectors.
    Then:
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
      \item among the completers $\Phi = \exp\sum_i h(\mu_i)$ built on a carrier
      $Y_\alpha = \ell_{\mathrm{sp}}^{2}\, g^{-1}(\mathrm{Ric} - \alpha R\, g)/(1 - \alpha d)$, with $\mu_i$ its
      eigenvalues and $h$ subject to the constraints of~\cite{Beau2026h} at the origin and at the saturation
      boundary, only $\alpha = 0$ satisfies (C3); the carrier is then $X$;
      \item the determinantal density of Eq.~\eqref{eq:BI-gravity}, with sector function
      $h(\lambda) = \tfrac12\log(1 + \lambda)$, is an admissible completion;
      \item it is not the only one: every $\Phi = \exp\sum_i h(\lambda_i)$ with $h$ subject to the same constraints
      is admissible, and the one-parameter family
      $\Phi_a(X) = \sqrt{\det(I + X)}\,\exp(a\,\mathrm{tr}\,X^2)$, $a \in \mathbb{R}$, satisfies (C1)--(C5) for
      every $a$ and differs from the determinantal member at second order in the infrared expansion.
    \end{enumerate}
  \end{proposition}

  \begin{proof}
    This is the theorem on admissibility and non-uniqueness of the tensorial completion of~\cite{Beau2026h},
    restated without change of hypotheses or conclusion; its proof is given there.
    The selection $\alpha = 0$ in~(a) follows from the saturation condition (C3) applied to the curvature
    endomorphism, and the residual freedom in~(c) is the freedom of the sector function $h$.
    The additional input that a uniqueness result would require is stated in~\cite{Beau2026h}.
  \end{proof}

  \begin{remark}[Scope]
    Proposition~\ref{prop:BI-unique} classifies completions of an Einstein term that is supplied, not derived.
    It does not derive the Einstein--Hilbert term from $S_{\mathrm{coh}}$, does not select the determinantal member
    within the admissible family, and fixes neither $\mathcal{N}$, nor $\ell_{\mathrm{sp}}$, nor the Newton coupling.
    It is conditional on the open Hypothesis~[H-ext].
  \end{remark}
